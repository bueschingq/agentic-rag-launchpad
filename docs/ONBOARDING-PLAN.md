# Onboarding plan: a client live in under 30 minutes

## Objective

A partner (MSP or agency) onboards a new client and, **within 30 minutes**, has a chatbot on the client's site that:

- answers from the site's own content
- looks like the site
- is ready for the client's marketing team

The clock starts when the partner clicks **Add customer**. It stops when the widget answers a real question, with sources, on the client's staging or live site.

Fast must not mean thin. Everything a digital marketer expects from a site chatbot belongs in the product, and Sitefinity sites, the partners' most common platform, get first-class treatment.

## Sitefinity first

Sitefinity sites come up in most partner engagements, so the product handles their specifics directly:

- **Detection** from `data-sf-` attributes and Sitefinity resource paths, since the generator meta tag is often stripped (found on crayola.com in M0). The version isn't exposed, so the wizard asks for it with "not sure" as an option.
- **Content path:**
  - On 15.4 and later, Sitefinity's own Agentic RAG connector syncs content from the CMS. The product shows the three values the connector needs (endpoint without `/api`, KB UID, writer key), each with a copy button.
  - On older versions, a sitemap sync. Sitefinity's sitemap module publishes `/sitemap/sitemap.xml` and can hold thousands of pages (crayola.com: 5,438), so a page budget and include/exclude paths are required.
- **Content quality:** sync reads the page's main content, not the header, navigation and footer that every page repeats, using the content placeholder of Sitefinity layouts by default.
- **Widget path:** `<arag-chat>` (themed, all features) or Sitefinity's native AI Assistant widget for clients who want it managed in the CMS.
- **Embedding:** the snippet goes in the layout template once, or in a Script widget on chosen pages, in both the MVC and the ASP.NET Core renderers. It's one async script, so it doesn't block rendering. Content Security Policy entries are listed for sites that use one.
- **Multisite and cultures:** one deployment per site; culture-specific URLs (`/en/`, `/es/`) map to the deployment's language or to "each visitor's language".
- **Sitefinity Insight:** chat events can be sent to Insight as interactions, alongside GA.

| Widget (`<arag-chat>`) | Four layouts, theme, handoff, CTAs, GA, contact form, thumbs, page exclusions, per-language copy, the confirmed price table checked live on price questions, the shared renderer for model text, 44.7 KB gzipped (measured at each build; 2026-11-01) | Proactive greeting, Insight events. The budget is 45 KB (raised from 40 KB on 2026-10-31)                                      |
| Agentic RAG            | KB, keys, saved prompt, ask, analytics                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Link resources for synced pages (writer key); question log and REMi for reporting                                              |

Content sync sends each page's extracted text as a text resource, keyed by a slug from its URL, rather than as a link resource or through the server-side sitemap connector. Measured 2026-10-30: link resources took 220–235 s per page, text resources a few seconds. The worker controls the page budget, the include/exclude rules and re-syncs, and every page carries its URL for citations.

## Why this plan is good

1. **It shortens the wait, not the work.** Provisioning already takes seconds. What takes time is content processing and human review, so the plan runs content sync and brand review **in parallel** and starts answering as soon as the first pages are indexed.
2. **Every step has a default.** The scan proposes the theme, the template proposes the prompt, and the sitemap proposes the content. The partner confirms rather than authors, which is what makes 30 minutes repeatable by any staff member, not only the expert.
3. **Validation is part of onboarding.** Smoke-test questions with sources and a domain-lock check mean "done" is observed, not assumed. That is what lets a partner hand a client a working link with confidence.
4. **Marketing features are in from day one.** Conversion (CTAs, handoff, lead capture), measurement (GA, Insight) and reporting are what make the chatbot a marketing asset a client keeps paying for, rather than a demo.
5. **Sitefinity specifics are in the product rather than the runbook.** Detection, connector values, layout and Script-widget embedding, and content-zone extraction come from M0 findings on real Sitefinity sites, not assumptions.
6. **It stays safe.** Keys are sealed at rest and browsers only get 15-minute reader tokens. KBs are domain-locked, links are allow-listed twice, and analytics never receive visitor text.
7. **It's measurable.** The onboarding timer, smoke-test results and quality scores turn the 30-minute promise into a number per client.

## Build order

1. **Content sync** (worker): sitemap budget, include/exclude, main-content selector, progress, scheduled re-sync. Without content there is no chatbot, so this comes first.
2. **One wizard:** detection and plan review, then provision, sync, scan and apply the theme, with progress on one screen.
3. **Validation:** generated starter questions, smoke test, domain-lock polling, onboarding timer.
4. **Go-live hand-off:** Sitefinity steps, connector values, printable client sheet.
5. **Marketer features:** page targeting, proactive greeting, lead capture, feedback thumbs, Insight events, prompt templates, reporting.

Each step is reviewed by four personas (partner executive, sales engineer, marketer, UX designer) before the next round of work is chosen.
